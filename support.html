How do I cancel MicroChef Pro?

Apple handles the billing, so cancellation happens on your device: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MicroChef → Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve already paid for.

I subscribed but the app still shows the paywall

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore purchases, making sure you’re signed into the same Apple ID you bought with. If it still doesn’t unlock, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I delete my account?

Open your profile (the person icon, top right) and choose Delete account. It removes everything — recipes, diary, photos, XP, friends — and can’t be undone. Cancel your subscription separately, in Settings, since Apple bills it.

The ingredient scan got something wrong

Tap any ingredient to edit it, or add what it missed, before generating. The recipes are built from the corrected list.

I’ve hit my daily generation limit

Free accounts get a set number of AI recipe generations each day; it resets at midnight. MicroChef Pro removes the limit.

Where’s the Social tab?

Leagues unlock at kitchen level 2 and require MicroChef Pro.
