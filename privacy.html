MicroChef

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

MicroChef is a cooking app: you photograph the ingredients you have, it suggests recipes built around them, and it tracks what you eat. This explains what we collect to do that, who it is shared with, and how to get rid of it.

We do not sell your information.MicroChef has no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking. We do not share your information for advertising.

Photos

When you scan ingredients or food, the photo is uploaded to our storage and sent to Google’s Gemini API, which identifies what is in it. Recipe requests send your ingredient list, goal, calorie target, dietary restrictions, spices, pantry items and appliances. We do not send your name or email. Google handles this content under Google’s API terms. We ask your permission in the app before the first photo or recipe request is sent, and you can turn it off at any time in Profile. We keep photos until you delete them or delete your account. We do not use your photos to train any model.

AI features

Recipes, ingredient recognition and nutrition numbers are produced by AI. They can be wrong, so check ingredients yourself, especially if you have an allergy. Nothing is decided about you by automated means that has legal or similarly significant effects. Generated dish pictures are stored in a public image folder, so anyone who has the link to a picture can open it. They show food, not you.

Health and nutrition information

Food diary entries, calorie and macro goals, height, weight, age, sex, activity level and dietary restrictions are sensitive. We use them only to run the features you ask for. We do not sell them or use them for advertising. If you join a coach’s group using their code, that coach can see your goals, food diary and streaks. That is the purpose of the feature, and you can leave the group at any time from your profile.

Consumer Health Data

This section is our consumer health data notice for people in Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and similar states.

  • What we collect: food and calorie diary entries, calorie and macro goals, height, weight, age, sex, activity level, dietary restrictions, and photos of food.
  • Why: to calculate and track your goals and to generate recipes. Nothing else.
  • Where it comes from: only from you.
  • Who gets it: our service providers (Supabase for storage, Google Gemini for processing photos and recipe requests) and, only if you join their group, your coach. We do not sell it.
  • Your rights: to confirm we collect it, to see it, to withdraw consent and to delete it. You can also appeal a refusal. Email us and, if you disagree with our answer to an appeal, you can contact your state’s Attorney General.
  • Consent: you choose what to enter. You can withdraw by deleting the entries, turning off AI features in Profile, deleting your account, or emailing us.

Who your data is shared with

Only the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage.
  • Google Gemini: analyses ingredient photos and generates recipes. It receives the photo or ingredient list for that request.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: process and validate subscription purchases. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and subscription status.
  • Apple and Google: if you use their sign-in, they confirm who you are to us.
  • Railway: runs our API server.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the public copies of this policy and the Terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We may disclose information if legally required to.

Why we use your data (EU and UK)

  • Account, diary, recipes and purchases: to perform our contract with you.
  • Security and abuse prevention: our legitimate interests.
  • Health-type data and AI processing of photos: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw in the app or by email.
  • Age and terms record: legal obligation and our legitimate interests.

California and other US states

Notice at collection. In the last 12 months we collected: identifiers (email, name, account ID); app activity (usage counters, XP and streaks); photos; health and diet information (sensitive); purchase status; and inferences used only to run features. We collect them to provide the app. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use sensitive information beyond providing the service, so there is nothing to opt out of. You may ask us to tell you what we hold, delete it, or correct it, and you can use an authorised agent. We do not treat you differently for asking. Other users

If you take part in leagues or add friends, other members can see your display name, level and XP. They cannot see your email address. Your food diary, photos and body measurements are not visible to them. A coach you join can see your goals, food diary and streaks.

You can report and block other users. We review reports within 24 hours, and names that are objectionable are removed. support@microchef.app

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your database rows and uploaded files straight away. Copies in routine provider backups and logs are overwritten on the provider’s normal schedule. RevenueCat and Apple keep purchase records under their own policies. We keep the record of your age and terms confirmation only for as long as your account exists, and we may keep limited records where the law requires it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app: open your profile (the person icon, top right) and choose Delete account. This removes your profile, XP and streaks, saved recipes, diary entries and goals, spice cabinet and kitchen setup, uploaded photos, and your friend, league and coach links. It cannot be undone and we cannot recover it afterwards.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription, because Apple bills it, not us. Cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records.

Where your data is processed

Our providers store and process data in the United States and elsewhere, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. You can complain to your local data-protection authority.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose. Account deletion is in the app (Profile, then Delete account). For a copy of your data or anything else, email us. We reply within 30 days (45 days for California requests). You will not be treated differently for asking. support@microchef.app

Cookies and device storage

We do not use advertising cookies. The app stores a few small items on your device, for example how many recipes you generated today and which notices you have dismissed. Clearing the app’s storage removes them.

Children

MicroChef is for people 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. We ask everyone to confirm their age when creating an account, and we remove the account of anyone who says they are under 13. If you are 13 to 17, you need a parent or guardian’s permission. A parent who thinks a child under 13 has an account can email us and we will delete it. support@microchef.app

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting providers. Access to your rows is limited to your account at the database level, and payment details never reach our servers. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and regulators of a breach where the law requires it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app. If the AI section changes materially, we will ask for your permission again.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: support@microchef.app
