MicroChef

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

MicroChef is a cooking app: you photograph the ingredients you have, it suggests recipes built around them, and it tracks what you eat. This explains what we collect to do that, who it is shared with, and how to get rid of it.

We do not track you.MicroChef contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers. Nothing you do in the app is sold, shared with data brokers, or used to build an advertising profile.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and nameTo create and sign you into your account. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever receive Apple’s relay address.
Account identifierTo link your data to your account and to attribute a subscription purchase to you.
Photos you take or selectTo identify the ingredients in them. See “Photos” below.
Nutrition and body dataYour food diary entries, calorie and macro goals, and the height, weight, age, sex and activity level you enter into the BMR calculator, so the app can set and track targets.
Cooking dataSaved recipes, spice cabinet contents, appliances, dietary restrictions, XP, streaks and quest progress.
Social dataFriends, league membership and leaderboard position, and the coach–client link if you join a coach’s group with their code.
Purchase statusWhether you have an active MicroChef Pro subscription. We never see your card details — Apple handles payment.

Photos

When you scan ingredients, the photo is uploaded to our storage and sent to Google’s Gemini API to identify what is in it. The recognised ingredients are saved to your account. Photos are stored under your account and are deleted when you delete your account. We do not use your photos to train any model, and Google does not use API content to train its models.

Health and nutrition data

The nutrition and body measurements you enter are stored with your account so the app can calculate and track your goals. They are not shared with anyone except as described below, and they are never used for advertising. If you join a coach’s group using their code, that coach can see your nutrition progress — that is the purpose of the feature, and you can leave the group at any time from your profile.

Who your data is shared with

Only the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Google (Gemini API) — analyses ingredient photos and generates recipes. It receives the photo or ingredient list for that request.
  • Apple and RevenueCat — process and validate subscription purchases. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and subscription status.
  • Railway and Vercel — host the app and its backend.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We may disclose information if legally required to.

Other users

If you take part in leagues or add friends, your display name, level and XP are visible to the other people in that league or on your friends list. Your food diary, photos and body measurements are not.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. Delete your account and it is removed.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app: open your profile (the person icon, top right) and choose Delete account. This removes your profile, XP and streaks, saved recipes, diary entries and goals, spice cabinet and kitchen setup, uploaded photos, and your friend, league and coach links. It cannot be undone and we cannot recover it afterwards.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription, because Apple bills it, not us. Cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Most of this is available directly in the app; for anything else, contact us and we will respond.

Children

MicroChef is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to your rows is restricted to your account at the database level, and payment details never reach our servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app.
